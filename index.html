Why the White Butter

Not mythology recreated.
Emotion, made luminous.

"In Krishna's childhood, makhan was never just food. It was innocence, purity, warmth — unconditional love."

Brijwasis would churn butter through the night — not to store it, but secretly hoping Krishna would come and steal it. In Vrindavan, even stolen butter felt sacred. From that timeless feeling, The White Butter was imagined: a home as clear, as warm, and as quietly radiant as that memory.
